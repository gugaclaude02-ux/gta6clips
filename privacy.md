# Privacy Policy

GTA6 Clips Poster. Last updated: November 19, 2026.

## 1. Who uses the App

GTA6 Clips Poster is used only by the owner of the TikTok account @gta6.clipss01. It does not collect data from the public.

## 2. Data accessed

When the owner signs in with TikTok, the App receives basic account information, such as the display name and account ID, and an access token that allows it to upload videos to the account of the owner.

## 3. How data is used

This information is used only to upload the approved videos of the owner to the account of the owner. It is not sold, shared or used for advertising.

## 4. Storage

Access tokens are stored only on the personal computer of the owner and are never published or sent to third parties.

## 5. Deleting data

The owner can revoke the access of the App at any time in the TikTok settings. Doing so stops all access by the App.

## 6. Contact

Send a direct message to the TikTok account @gta6.clipss01
